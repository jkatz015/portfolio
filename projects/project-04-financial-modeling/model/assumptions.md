## Key Metrics to Track

### Profitability
- Gross Profit Margin (Target: 60%+)
- EBITDA Margin (Target: 12-15%)
- Net Profit Margin (Target: 5-8%)

### Liquidity
- Current Ratio (Target: 1.5+)
- Quick Ratio (Target: 1.0+)
- Days Cash on Hand (Target: 30+)

### Efficiency
- Prime Cost % (Target: <65%)
- Revenue per Seat (Target: $25K+/year)
- Inventory Turnover (Target: 50x/year)

### Leverage
- Debt-to-Equity (Target: <1.0)
- Interest Coverage (Target: 3x+)

## Sensitivity Variables

The model includes sensitivity analysis on:

1. **Revenue Growth** - What if growth is +/- 2% from assumptions?
2. **Food Cost %** - What if food costs increase 1-2%?
3. **Labor Cost %** - What if minimum wage increases?
